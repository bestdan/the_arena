### Teaser

Snak arrives looking... off. Not her usual blunt confidence. She's chewing on something she can't swallow.

> "Alright, champions."

She stops. Blinks at her own words.

> "Huh. Yeah. I mean it now. Anyway—"

She drops onto a crate, arms folded, and just sits there for a beat. That's new.

> "So. Next match. I got nothing."

Another beat.

> "Let me say that again so you understand how fucked up it is. I. Got. Nothing. Eight matches I've had you, and every time I've walked in here with a sketch, a name, a rumor—*something*. This time? My people don't know. The guards don't know. The bookmakers can't even set odds because nobody can explain the format."

She leans forward.

> "Here's what I *do* know. Some wizard from outside the Arena—not Arena staff, not Guild, *outside*—has been given the floor for this one. Some kind of joint venture with the Grand Master. Very hush hush, very prestigious, very 'aren't we clever.' They've been building in there for three days. I snuck a look through the service gate last night."

She pauses, like she's still processing what she saw.

> "The dirt's gone. The whole arena floor is... crystal? Smooth, shiny, like standing on a frozen lake that glows. There's this big clockwork thing in the center—gears, spinning arms, looks like one of those models astronomers use. And mirrors. Tall mirrors on the walls. I caught my own reflection and it moved wrong. Half a second behind me. Or ahead. I don't know."

She rubs her temples.

> "I keep hearing words I can't afford: 'Chrono-boros.' 'The Unresolved Paradox.' 'Temporal.' One of the construction guys said something about 'two eras' and then got real quiet when he saw me listening. Another one mentioned hourglasses—big ones, hanging from the ceiling. Two of them."

> "And here's the part that's got me twisted. I overheard one of the wizard's people say—and I'm quoting this exactly because it makes no fucking sense to me—'It's not about killing them. It's about which ones you kill.'"

She lets that hang.

> "In eight matches, nobody has ever said 'it's not about killing them' in this building. That's literally the only thing this building is *for*."

She stands back up, brushing off her vest.

> "I'll keep digging. I've still got two days. If anyone's got 100 gold, I'll turn over every stone I can find—but I'm warning you now, my usual sources are dry on this one. I'll have to go deeper, and what I find might be thinner than usual. Or weirder. Probably weirder."

She heads for the door, then stops.

> "One more thing. Whatever this is, the crowd doesn't know either. Buzz in the stands is all speculation—'time magic,' 'fighting your own past,' 'the arena changes mid-fight.' Could be bullshit. Could be all true. With wizards, you never fucking know."

> "Get your gold together. Talk to each other. And maybe... think about what you'd do if the rules aren't what you expect."

She leaves. The door shuts heavier than usual.
