# The Uninvited Guest

## Vignette: Valdris Kerne's Gift

**When to Run:** During the party's rest or planning phase before the arena battle.

---

### Scene Setup

The party is gathered—perhaps reviewing tactics, sharing a meal, or simply catching their breath before the coming trial. The torchlight flickers once, then dims. Shadows at the edge of the room seem to deepen unnaturally, pooling like spilled ink.

A figure steps from darkness that shouldn't exist.

### Read Aloud

> A lean man emerges from the shadows in the corner of the room—shadows that, a moment ago, were far too shallow to conceal anyone. He wears deep blue robes threaded with silver in geometric patterns that hurt to look at directly. His face is angular, ageless in the way of someone who has spent too long around magic. He holds a slim leather case under one arm.
>
> "Forgive the intrusion," he says, though his tone suggests he isn't particularly sorry. "I am ... well, it's probably better for everyone if you don't know that.  I represent... interests that would prefer your opponents in the arena not succeed quite so decisively."
>
> He sets the case on the nearest surface and opens it, revealing five distinct objects nestled in velvet.
>
> "I'm not your friend. Let's be clear on that. But right now, we share an opponent. These—" he gestures to the items "—will work once, perhaps twice. After that, they're trinkets. Use them well."
>
> His smile doesn't reach his eyes. "I'd hate to have wasted my investment."

### Roleplaying Valdris

- **Demeanor:** Polite, controlled, faintly amused. Never rude, but never warm.
- **Questions about his organization:** "That's not relevant to our arrangement."
- **Questions about payment:** "Let's call this a gesture of goodwill. I'm sure an opportunity for reciprocity will present itself."
- **Questions about the enemies:** He knows they're dangerous. He won't elaborate beyond: "They've disrupted certain... timelines of interest."
- **Threats or hostility:** He remains unruffled. "I could simply leave with these. Your choice."

### Exit

Once the items are accepted (or refused), Valdris inclines his head slightly.

> "Good luck in the arena. I'll be watching."

He steps backward into the shadows—and is simply gone. The darkness returns to normal depth. He was never there.

### Future Hook

Valdris clearly expects something in return. This debt can be called in during future sessions—a favor, information, or perhaps the party finds themselves opposing one of *his* enemies next.

# Magic Items

## Pinning Arrow

*Ammunition (arrow), uncommon*

This arrow's head is forged from cold iron and etched with binding runes that glow faintly blue when nocked.

When you hit a creature with this arrow, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be **restrained** until the end of its next turn as the arrow pins it to a nearby surface, the ground, or even its own shadow.

The arrow is destroyed upon use, whether the saving throw succeeds or fails.

**Gift includes:** 2 Pinning Arrows

---

## Lodestone of the Calm Eye

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

This palm-sized stone is perfectly smooth and unnaturally heavy for its size. Pale blue veins run through dark granite, pulsing faintly like a slow heartbeat. When held near active magic, the veins glow brighter.

As a reaction when you or a creature you can see within 30 feet is targeted by a spell or caught in a spell's area of effect, you can raise the lodestone and speak its command word. Choose up to three creatures within 30 feet of you (which can include yourself). Until the start of your next turn, chosen creatures have **resistance** to damage from spells.

Additionally, chosen creatures have **advantage** on saving throws against spells for the same duration.

The lodestone cracks and becomes inert after use.

---

## Martyr's Draught

*Potion, uncommon*

This small crystal vial contains a thick golden liquid that seems to glow with inner warmth. When held, you can faintly hear what sounds like a distant choir.

As a bonus action, you can drink this potion. For the next minute, whenever you cast a spell that restores hit points to a creature, you may choose one additional creature within 30 feet of you. That creature regains hit points equal to **half** the amount restored to the original target (rounded down).

This effect applies to each creature healed by the spell, potentially allowing multiple secondary targets if the original spell heals multiple creatures.

---

## Heartfire Brand

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

This small iron brand is marked with a stylized flame. When gripped firmly, it heats in your palm—uncomfortably warm, but not enough to burn.

As a bonus action while raging, you can press the brand to your weapon and speak its command word. For the next minute, whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, the target takes an additional **1d6 fire damage**, and you gain **temporary hit points** equal to the fire damage dealt.

These temporary hit points do not stack with themselves; each successful hit replaces any remaining temporary hit points from this effect.

The brand crumbles to rust after use.

---

## Meridian Beads

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a monk)*

This string of three jade prayer beads is worn wrapped around the wrist or woven into a sash. Each bead is carved with a different symbol representing breath, motion, and stillness.

When you spend ki points on an ability or feature that requires a creature to make a saving throw, and that creature **succeeds** on the saving throw, you may crush one of the beads as a free action (no action required). If you do, you regain the ki points spent on that failed attempt.

Each bead can only be used once. When all three beads have been crushed, the string becomes a mundane bracelet.
