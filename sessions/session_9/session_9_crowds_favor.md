# Session 9: Anti-Mage Crowd's Favor Options

These special Crowd's Favor options are available during the Chrono-boros encounter. They represent the crowd's instinctive resistance to the timeline-warping magic on display—their collective will pushing back against forces that feel fundamentally *wrong*.

**GM Note:** Announce these options are available at the start of combat or when the first major spell is cast. The party hasn't faced serious spellcasters before, so this gives them tools without requiring they anticipate the threat.

---

## New Options

### Arena's Aegis
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 3 Favor |
| **Category** | Defense |
| **Action** | Reaction |
| **Trigger** | You are targeted by a spell or in the area of a spell |
| **Effect** | Gain advantage on the saving throw against that spell. |

*The crowd's roar swells around you like a shield, their collective attention making you harder to touch.*

---

### Concentration Breaker
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 3 Favor |
| **Category** | Control |
| **Action** | Reaction |
| **Trigger** | You see a creature within 60 ft. maintaining concentration on a spell |
| **Effect** | The crowd's sudden, thunderous chant assaults their focus. The creature must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) or lose concentration. |

*"BREAK! BREAK! BREAK!" The arena shakes with a thousand voices in perfect, jarring unison.*

---

### Spellbreaker's Chant
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 4 Favor |
| **Category** | Control |
| **Action** | Reaction |
| **Trigger** | A creature within 60 ft. casts a spell of 2nd level or lower |
| **Effect** | The crowd's discordant chanting disrupts the weave. The spell fails and has no effect. The caster's spell slot is still expended. |

*The crowd begins a rhythmless, atonal chant that grates against the ears—and against magic itself.*

---

### Spotlight Blind
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 4 Favor |
| **Category** | Control |
| **Action** | Bonus |
| **Effect** | Choose a creature within 30 ft. Until the end of its next turn, the arena's magical lights blaze in its eyes. The creature has disadvantage on spell attack rolls, and other creatures have advantage on saving throws against its spells. |

*Every light in the arena pivots, focusing a blinding convergence on the caster.*

---

### The Crowd's Denial
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 5 Favor |
| **Category** | Control |
| **Action** | Reaction |
| **Trigger** | A creature you can see within 60 ft. casts a spell of 3rd level or lower |
| **Effect** | The crowd's collective will rejects the magic. The spell fails and has no effect. The caster's spell slot is still expended. |

*Ten thousand voices cry "NO!" in perfect unison. The spell unravels, its energy scattered by sheer defiance.*

---

### Silencing Ovation
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 6 Favor |
| **Category** | Control |
| **Action** | Bonus |
| **Effect** | Choose a point within 60 ft. The crowd's thunderous cheering creates a 20-foot radius zone of magical interference centered on that point. The zone lasts until the start of your next turn. While in the zone, creatures cannot cast spells with verbal components, and concentration checks are made with disadvantage. |

*The crowd rises as one, stamping and screaming. In that cacophony, no incantation can be heard—not even by the magic itself.*

## Tactical Notes for GM

- **Spellbreaker's Chant (4)** and **The Crowd's Denial (5)** are the counterspell equivalents. Price them at the value of completely negating a dangerous spell.
- **Spotlight Blind (4)** is excellent against the Chrono-Mage Ascendant's spell attacks and AoE spells—consider hinting at this if players are struggling.
- **Silencing Ovation (6)** can shut down a caster for a full round but requires significant favor investment. Best used during a timeline where the strong mage is active.
- The Chrono-Mage Ascendant's key concentration spells are *Slow* and *Haste*. **Concentration Breaker** at 3 favor is an efficient answer.
