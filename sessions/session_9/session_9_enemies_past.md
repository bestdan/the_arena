
### Past Timeline Enemies

#### Temporal Knight Prime (Strong Version, CR 5)
**The Legend**

> **D&D Beyond Base:** [Gladiator](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16905-gladiator) (MM p346)
> **Modifications:** AC 18 (plate + shield), longsword + 1d6 force instead of spear/shield bash, add legendary actions and Guardian's Oath

The Knight in his legendary prime, decades of battle experience. Protects the Apprentice with deadly efficiency.

**Stats:**
- **HP:** 112
- **AC:** 18 (plate armor + shield)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +0

**Abilities:**
- STR 18 (+4), DEX 10 (+0), CON 16 (+3), INT 10 (+0), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 14 (+2)

**Saves:**
- STR +7, CON +6

**Skills:**
- Athletics +7, Intimidation +5

**Attacks:**
- **Multiattack:** Makes three longsword attacks.
- **Longsword:** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d8+4 slashing (or 1d10+4 two-handed) + 1d6 force damage (temporal strike).
- **Shield Bash (Bonus Action):** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d4+4 bludgeoning damage, target pushed 5 feet.

**Legendary Actions (3/round):**
Only available when within 30 feet of Apprentice Mage (protecting his past self).

- **Protective Parry (1 action):** Impose disadvantage on one attack roll against Apprentice Mage within 10 feet.
- **Guardian's Advance (1 action):** Move up to speed toward Apprentice without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Temporal Slash (2 actions):** Make one longsword attack.

**Special Abilities:**

**Guardian's Oath:**
- When within 10 feet of Apprentice Mage:
  - Advantage on attack rolls
  - +2 AC (AC 20 total)
  - Represents perfect synergy protecting his younger self

**Rage (Triggers when Apprentice dies):**
- If Apprentice drops to 0 HP and is not rescued
- Gains advantage on all attack rolls
- Resistance to all damage
- Loses legendary actions (consumed by fury)
- Represents grief and determination to prevent temporal paradox

**Tactics:**
- **Positioning:** Stays within 10 feet of Apprentice (Guardian's Oath active)
- **Shield Bash:** Push melee PCs away from Apprentice aggressively
- **Legendary Actions:** Use Protective Parry to intercept attacks on Apprentice
- **Guardian's Advance:** If separated, immediately close distance to Apprentice
- **Temporal Slash:** Use when Apprentice is safe, to pressure PCs
- **Multiattack:** Three longsword attacks per turn (avg 27-30 damage/round)
- **Prioritizes protection:** Will not chase PCs who retreat, stays near Apprentice
- **If Apprentice dies:** Enters Rage, becomes reckless aggressor
- **With 5 PCs:** Will be flanked more easily, must use Shield Bash to create space

#### Chrono-Mage Apprentice (Weak Version, CR 2)
**The Student**

The Ascendant's past self, still learning magic. Vulnerable but supported by the legendary Knight.

**Stats:**
- **HP:** 35
- **AC:** 12 (15 with *mage armor*, which he casts pre-battle)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +2

**Abilities:**
- STR 8 (-1), DEX 14 (+2), CON 10 (+0), INT 16 (+3), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 10 (+0)

**Saves:**
- INT +5, WIS +3

**Skills:**
- Arcana +5, History +5

**Spells (Spell Save DC 13, +5 to hit):**
- **Cantrips:** *ray of frost*, *minor illusion*, *prestidigitation*
- **1st level (4 slots):** *mage armor* (pre-cast), *magic missile*, *shield*, *expeditious retreat*
- **2nd level (2 slots):** *misty step*, *hold person*

**Special Abilities:**

**Temporal Anchor (1/combat):**
- When reduced to 0 HP, freezes in time for 1 round (petrified condition)
- If Temporal Knight Prime reaches him and uses action, Apprentice stabilizes at 1 HP
- Represents knight's desperate attempt to save his past self
- If not rescued within 1 round, Apprentice dies

**Tactics:**
- **Positioning:** Stays behind Knight Prime, uses him as mobile cover
- **Control:** Casts *hold person* on biggest melee threat (DC 13 Wis save)
- **Damage:** Uses *magic missile* to chip away at PCs (auto-hit, 3 darts × 1d4+1)
- **Defense:** Casts *shield* reactively when targeted (+5 AC until start of his next turn, AC 20 total)
- **Escape:** If cornered, uses *misty step* to teleport behind altar (full cover)
- **Maintains distance:** Avoids melee at all costs, relies on Knight's protection
- **Temporal Anchor:** Activates automatically when reduced to 0 HP
- **With 5 PCs:** More likely to be flanked, relies heavily on Knight's positioning
