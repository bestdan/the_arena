# Session 10 Encounter: Kiddie Chaos

## Kiddie Chaos

**Party:** [[imwe|Imwe]] (Fighter 5), [[father_crow|Father Crow]] (Cleric 5), [[torgana|Torgana]] (Barbarian 5), **{{FOURTH PC — name, class}}** (Level 5)
**Objective:** Survive the bout and put down Bassa's crew while the Arena is bent nine times by three children you cannot reach, cannot talk to, and cannot blame.

**Source:** `ideas/kiddie_chaos.md`

---

## Arena Layout

The floor is dressed as a broken market square. Stalls with cloth awnings, a dry stone fountain, and a bell tower fifteen feet tall at the center. Standard twenty-foot walls, four gates.

Thirty feet up the north wall hangs the **Little Box** — gilded, cushioned, warded, with three children in it and a magister standing behind them who touches nothing.

### Key Features
- **Market stalls** (eight, scattered): half cover, and a creature inside one is cramped. Flimsy — a stall collapses if a Large or bigger creature enters it.
- **Dry fountain** (center-west, 15 ft across, 4 ft deep): full cover from outside, difficult terrain to climb out of. Becomes a canyon when everyone is *reduced*.
- **Bell tower** (center, 15 ft): climbable at half speed, a 5-ft platform on top, and a bell that still works. Someone will ring it. Let them.
- **The walls**: twenty feet, warded to the top. Under Reverse Gravity they are the only thing between the fighters and the sky.
- **The Little Box**: unreachable. Attacks and spells aimed at it fail against the same ward-stone that keeps the crowd safe. Do not let a PC talk themselves into a plan that ends at the Box.
- **The conduit**: a hairline seam of glyph-light down the inside of the north wall, ending at a brass junction plate at floor level behind the north gate. Ankle-high. Unwarded. Nobody looks at it. See [Killing the Box](#killing-the-box).

### Environmental Rules
- The bout runs the full floor — no shrinking ring, no timer.
- Everything the Box does happens to **everyone on the sand**, including Bassa's crew. Say this out loud in round one so the party stops treating it as an attack.
- The crowd is loud enough that shouted tactics work fine. Until they don't.

## Special Mechanic: The Little Box

Three consoles. Three brass buttons each. **Nine buttons, one press each, one press per round**, at the top of the round before initiative count 20.

A child cannot aim, cannot choose a target, and cannot un-press. When a button is pressed, its stamp **flares on the outer face of the Box** so the whole stadium sees which one went — that is the only information the sand ever gets.

Keep this side of the screen.

| Console | Symbol | Effect | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Vessia | **Sun** | **Everyone Big** | All creatures *enlarged* 1 min. Stalls become knee-high. Anyone in a stall or the tower takes 1d6 and is shoved prone; that stall collapses. |
| I — Vessia | **Pine** | **The Hush** | All sound stops for 1 min — *silence*, wall to wall. No spell with a verbal component. No shouted warning, name, or plan. Every creature on the sand is deafened. Thunder damage does nothing. |
| I — Vessia | **Crescent** | **Reverse Gravity** | Everything on the sand falls up for 1 min. DC 14 Dex to catch a fixture and hang on. Falling creatures strike the ward-field at 20 ft for 2d6 and hang there. Ends without warning — everyone falls. |
| II — Ottavan | **Anchor** | **Slow** | Everyone on the sand *slowed* (DC 15 Wis negates) for 3 rounds. The crowd counts each action aloud. |
| II — Ottavan | **Wave** | **Downpour** | Rain and mud for the rest of the bout: ranged attacks at disadvantage, open ground is difficult terrain, and lightning hits one random combatant at the end of each round for 3d10 (DC 15 Dex half). |
| II — Ottavan | **Star** | **Random Polymorph** | Roll a d4 of random combatants; each becomes a CR 1-2 beast for 1 min. DC 15 Wis negates. Beast table below. |
| III — Pip | **Bell** | **Mirror Skins** | For 1 min every creature on the sand looks identical — same grey fighter, same face. Attacks against a creature you did not touch last round are at disadvantage. |
| III — Pip | **Coin** | **Everyone Small** | All creatures *reduced* 1 min. The fountain is a canyon. Weapon damage drops a die. |
| III — Pip | **Key** | **The Shuffle** | Every creature on the sand teleports to a random other creature's space. Roll it openly. No save. |

**Beast table for Star** (d6): 1 Giant Toad · 2 Giant Constrictor Snake · 3 Brown Bear · 4 Giant Vulture · 5 Elk · 6 Giant Octopus (flops; it is very funny and the crowd loves it).

### Bell into Key
If Mirror Skins is running when the Shuffle lands, nobody on the sand knows who anyone is — identical faces, scrambled positions. Let the table sit in it for a full round before anyone swings. It is the best moment the bout has. If Pip is yours to steer, steer her here.

### Who presses, and when
Do not roll this. Hand it to a player. See the GM setup for how.

- **Vessia** presses when the crowd is loudest, not when the fight needs it.
- **Ottavan** presses on whoever is winning.
- **Pip** presses when someone she likes is about to die.

## Killing the Box

The Box is unreachable. The wiring is not.

- **Spot it:** DC 14 Perception within 30 ft of the north wall, or DC 12 Arcana/Investigation from anywhere if the PC watches an effect resolve and traces where the light came from. Arcane background: advantage.
- **Reach it:** floor level behind the north gate. No climb — but it is the most exposed square on the sand, and the Wall-Huggers know exactly what it is.
- **Break it:** AC 17, 25 HP, resistant to nonmagical damage.
- **Unwire it:** DC 17 Arcana or Thieves' Tools, one action. Failure by 5+ means 2d8 lightning and the tools weld to the plate.
- **Smashing surges.** Break the plate and every unused button on that console fires at once, in any order you like, in that round. Then that console is dead. Unwiring cleanly kills it silent, no surge.
- **Crowd verdict:** **−3 [[crowds_favor|Favor]]**. Unless the attempt was made [[panache|with Panache]] and succeeded — then the mob decides it was the best thing they saw all year and it is **+3** instead. No middle result.
- **The child watches you do it.** Ottavan will remember. Pip will cry, and ten thousand people will see her cry.

## Enemies

### Composition

**Bassa the Unbothered** — Gladiator (CR 5). 112 HP, AC 16.
Veteran of four Kiddie Chaos bouts. Fights flat-footed and patient. She is not fast and does not need to be.
- *Multiattack:* three attacks — spear (+7, 2d6+4) or shield bash (+7, 2d4+4, DC 15 Str or prone).
- *Parry* (reaction): +3 AC against one melee attack.
- **Doctrine:** on any round a stamp flares, Bassa **Dodges** and lets it pass over her. She resumes attacking the round after. This makes her slower than her CR suggests and much harder to kill.
- **Voice:** she does not taunt. She says "again" when she is hit and nothing else all bout.

**2 Wall-Huggers** — Veteran (CR 3). 58 HP each, AC 17.
- *Multiattack:* two longsword (+5, 1d8+3) or heavy crossbow (+3, 1d10+1).
- **Doctrine:** never stand in open ground, never stand under the rigging, always within reach of a fixture. They **automatically succeed** the Reverse Gravity catch save, and they are never the ones caught inside a stall when Everyone Big lands.
- **One of them stands between the party and the junction plate at all times.** If a PC moves toward the north gate, that Wall-Hugger disengages from whatever it is doing and goes to the plate.

**4 Stall-Rats** — Thug (CR 1/2). 32 HP each, AC 11.
- *Multiattack:* two maces (+4, 1d6+2). *Pack Tactics.*
- **Doctrine:** fight from inside the stalls, scatter the instant a stamp flares on the Box. They are cowards with a job. They will not chase a PC into the open.

**Total:** 6 enemies. This is over the deadly line on paper for four level-5 PCs, and it is meant to be — the Box hits Bassa's crew as hard as it hits the party, and four Motes of Refusal are four guaranteed escapes. If the chaos rolls kind to the enemies twice running, cut a Stall-Rat between rounds and do not tell anyone.

### Enemy Tactics
- Round 1: Stall-Rats swarm whoever is most isolated. Wall-Huggers take the flanks along the wall. Bassa walks — walks — to the center.
- Bassa targets whichever PC the crowd is loudest for. She is here to kill a favorite, and she knows the favorite is the one the children are watching.
- The crew has fought this game before. They do not panic when an effect lands. **The party's advantage is that they will improvise and Bassa's crew will not** — the Wall-Huggers' doctrine is rigid, and a PC who notices that can exploit it (bait one away from its fixture, then press Crescent's aftermath).
- Nobody on the enemy side goes near the junction plate to *use* it. They guard it because the magisters pay them to.

## Round-by-Round Escalation

The children press at the top of each round. One per round. That is the clock.

| Round | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | No press — the magisters make the crowd wait. Fighting starts clean so the party learns the board. |
| 2 | **First press.** Whoever the table picked. Announce the symbol, resolve loudly, then straight back to initiative. |
| 3-4 | Presses land. Stall-Rats start dropping. Bassa reaches the party. |
| 5 | If nobody has looked at the north wall yet, give a free hint: an effect resolves and Father Crow (or the most arcane-adjacent PC) notices the light *came from somewhere* — offer the DC 12 trace. |
| 6-8 | Buttons burn down. The good ones are gone; what is left is what is left. Bassa is still standing. |
| 9-10 | Last presses. If the party has not engaged the junction plate, they never will — let it go and finish clean. |

**Do not stack effects into a mush.** One press, resolved clean, described loudly, back to the fight. If a press lands mid-Hush, resolve it in silence and let the table feel how bad that is.

### Victory Condition
Bassa the Unbothered drops or yields. She yields at 15 HP or lower if the crowd is loud for the party — she is a professional and this is an annual show, not a grudge. If the party kills her after she yields, that is **−2 Favor** and a name they will hear again.

### Secondary Objective: Win a child over
**Reward:** +1 [[crowds_favor|Favor]] on the successful approach, and that child presses for you at least once. Post-bout, a household takes an interest — see Aftermath.

### Tertiary Objective: Kill the Box
**Reward:** +3 Favor with Panache, −3 without. A very large problem with the magisters, arriving two sessions from now.

## Crowd Favor Opportunities

- Play to a child directly — bow, salute, throw them a trophy — landed [[panache|with Panache]]: **+1**, and that child presses next round regardless of the other two.
- Turn a Console effect into a kill in the same round it lands: **+2**.
- Land a Panache roll during the Hush, on pure physical flourish with nothing said: **+2**.
- Spend your mote to survive something the crowd was certain would kill you: **+2**.
- Spend your mote early and cheap, then win anyway: **+1**, and the crowd starts the chant.
- Save one of Bassa's crew from a Console effect: **+2**. The mob adores mercy it did not expect.
- Ring the bell in the tower at a moment that lands: **+1**.
- Kill a console: **+3** with Panache, **−3** without.
- Sulk, freeze, or blame the Box out loud: **−1**. The children are innocent. Say otherwise on the sand and find out.
- End the bout with an unspent Mote of Refusal: **−1**.

## Motes of Refusal

Handed to every combatant at the gate — a chalk-white bead. The betting houses insisted; a bout that is pure noise cannot be wagered on.

- **One per PC. One use. No recharge.** Reaction, spent when a spell or a Console effect would affect you.
- You are simply not affected. Everyone else still is.
- **It refuses the magic, not the world.** Refuse Reverse Gravity and you stay on the sand while the fight goes up — and the fight comes back down on top of you.
- Refuse an effect already running and you shed it. You cannot pre-spend it.
- Bassa holds one too, if you want the extra beat. She spends it on the loudest effect, out of spite.

## GM Tips

- **Have a real person pick the symbol.** Point at a player whose character is unconscious, or at whoever is loudest, and say "you're Pip — pick a symbol nobody's pressed." Do not roll it. The chaos should have a face.
- Play the children as children. They giggle. They lean over the rail. They point.
- Say out loud in round one that the effects hit everyone. Players will otherwise spend three rounds treating the Box as an enemy attack.
- **Pip and [[last_stand|Last Stand]]:** the moment a PC drops into Last Stand, Pip's hand goes to the brass. Let the table see her reach. Whether it saves them is not up to her.
- Let the party find the junction plate if they look for it. A party that works it out has earned whatever ending they get.
- The Hush is the hardest effect to run and the best one. Enforce it: no table talk in character, no shouted plans, no verbal spellcasting. Give them thirty seconds of real silence at the table if you can hold it.
- Four PCs, three children. One PC gets nobody. Do not fix this. Let the announcer notice it out loud.

## Scaling Options

### Too Easy?
Give Bassa a Mote of Refusal and legendary resistance 1/day. Add two Stall-Rats.

### Too Hard?
Cut a Wall-Hugger. Have Bassa's Dodge doctrine cost her the whole round instead of just her attacks. Pip presses early and generously.

### Too Little Chaos?
Two presses in a round, once, at round 6. The announcer calls it a "magisters' gift to the crowd."

### Too Much Chaos?
A child hesitates and skips a round. Vessia would. Pip absolutely would.

## Aftermath Seeds

- **The scandal:** a broadsheet counts the winners over nine years — twenty-four of twenty-seven from six families. Nobody cares. Somebody wants the party to make them care.
- **Pip:** her family is quietly evicted a week later, or quietly enriched, depending on whether her last press cost the wrong house money. Either way a PC can find her.
- **The mote trade:** an unspent Mote of Refusal is worth real coin on the [[blackmarket|black market]]. A fighter who palms one instead of dropping it at the gate is stealing from the betting houses, and they audit.
- **Patronage:** House Marn-Doru offers a contract to whichever PC Vessia decided was hers. The terms are generous. The terms are also a leash.
- **Sabotage:** somebody proves a console was pre-loaded. The evidence points at a magister, then past them, at a name much larger.
- **Bassa:** if she yielded and lived, she is the closest thing to a professional peer the party has met. She does not want friends. She would take a drink.
