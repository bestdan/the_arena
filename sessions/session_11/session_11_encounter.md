# Session 11 Encounter: Polymorphic Chaos

## The Transformation Trial

**Party:** 4 PCs, level 5. No NPC allies — the academies insisted the floor stay "uncontaminated."
**Objective:** Defeat Magister Ilvane Corr and his menagerie. Survive the Fonts, or embrace them.
**Expected Outcome:** 7-10 rounds. Hard, escalating to deadly if the party ignores the Fonts and lets the Magister keep concentration on a polymorph. At least one PC should spend a stretch of this fight as a bear.

---

## Arena Layout

The floor has been sunk into a circular pit, eighty feet across, ringed by three concentric bands of pale chalk that the academies painted to mark "shimmer distance" for the betting public. The chalk means nothing mechanically — it exists so the crowd can see who is standing somewhere stupid.

Three **Polymorphic Fonts** stand at the 12, 4, and 8 o'clock positions of the middle ring: crystalline pillars ten feet tall, humming at a pitch that sets teeth aching. Between them, bronze statues of past champions and slabs of quarried stone provide half cover. Nothing here can be moved. Everything here can be hidden behind.

### Key Features
- **Circular pit**, 80-foot diameter, flat sand. No difficult terrain.
- **Three Fonts** at 12, 4, and 8 o'clock on the middle ring (roughly 35 feet from center, and roughly 45 feet from each other).
- **Shimmer zones**: 20-foot radius around each Font. These overlap the center of the arena only slightly — a PC standing dead center is *outside* all three.
- **Cover**: six bronze statues and four stone slabs scattered through the middle and outer rings, half cover, immovable.
- **The gates**: north gate (PC entry), south gate (enemy entry). Both barred once the bout begins.

### Environmental Rules
- Standing in a shimmer zone is entirely optional. Every PC choice in this fight is a choice about whether to risk the shimmer for position.
- The Fonts are objects: **AC 15, 30 HP, immune to poison and psychic damage.** They can be attacked, shoved against, climbed.
- Destroying a Font stops its pulses permanently and releases a **15-foot-radius shimmer wave**: every transformed creature within range reverts to its true form immediately.

## Special Mechanic: The Wild Shimmer

This is a house effect, not the *polymorph* spell. Run it exactly as written below and you never need to look anything up.

### Pulses
- The Fonts pulse **at the end of rounds 3, 6, and 9**, simultaneously.
- Every creature within 20 feet of a pulsing Font makes a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw**.
- On a failure, roll **d6 on the Beast Forms table**. The creature takes that form.
- A creature may **choose to fail** the save. This is a real tactical option and the crowd adores it.

### Being Transformed
- You use the form's **AC, HP pool, speed, attacks, and physical ability scores**.
- You keep your own **Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma**, and your own mental saving throws. You are still you in there.
- **Track the form's HP as a separate pool.** Damage comes off that pool first. When it hits 0 you snap back into your own body with the hit points you had when you shifted, and any excess damage carries over to you.
- You **cannot** cast spells, use class features, or speak a language.
- You **can** still spend [[../../mechanics/crowds_favor|Crowd's Favor]] and roll [[../../mechanics/panache|with Panache]]. Say so out loud at the table the first time someone shifts — otherwise a player will assume they've been benched.
- The form ends when its HP reaches 0, when its Font is destroyed within 15 feet, or when the bout ends.

### The Magister's Polymorph Is Different
Ilvane's *polymorph* is a **concentration** spell. If he loses concentration, his victim reverts instantly. Make sure the players discover this — one hit on the caster is worth more than ten swings at a bear. The Fonts, by contrast, require no concentration and cannot be dispelled; only destruction stops them.

## Enemies

### Composition
- **1 Magister Ilvane Corr**, Transmuter of the Cassine Academy (CR 6)
- **1 Whimsy**, the Chaos Hound (CR 3)
- **3 Trial Gladiators** (CR 1/2 each, until the first pulse — then they are beasts)
- **3 Polymorphic Fonts** (objects, but the real opponent)

Raw XP ≈ 3,300, which sits just under a moderate day for four level-5 characters on paper. It plays harder than it reads, because the party will spend rounds at reduced capability and the Fonts turn the gladiators into CR 1-2 creatures partway through. Do not add more bodies before you see round 4.

### Magister Ilvane Corr (Mage, CR 6)
A slight man in academy grey with ink-stained cuffs, who keeps a running commentary on his own work as though the arena were a lecture hall.

- **AC 15** (*mage armor*), **HP 40**, Speed 30 ft
- **Spell save DC 14**, **+6 to hit** with spell attacks
- **Cantrips:** *fire bolt* (+6, 2d10 fire), *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*
- **1st (4 slots):** *shield*, *magic missile*
- **2nd (3):** *misty step*, *blur*
- **3rd (3):** *counterspell*, *fly*
- **4th (3):** ***polymorph*** ×3 (concentration), *greater invisibility*
- **5th (1):** *telekinesis*

**Tactics.** Opens by polymorphing the largest threat on the floor — Torgana, almost certainly — into something with no thumbs. Keeps 40+ feet from every Font; he has no intention of being transformed on his own stage. Uses *telekinesis* to drag a PC into a shimmer zone in the round before a pulse, which is the single nastiest thing he does. *Misty step* out of melee, *shield* against the first big hit, *blur* once bloodied.

**Yield.** At 12 HP or fewer he casts *greater invisibility* and runs for the south gate, shouting that the trial's data is "already sufficient." The crowd will *hate* this. The party can chase him — leaving transformed gladiators loose behind them — or let him go and clear the floor. Either choice is worth playing out; neither is wrong.

### Whimsy, the Chaos Hound (Hell Hound reskin, CR 3)
A lean, smoke-colored hound whose coat changes hue with each breath. The academies think it's charming. It is not.

- **AC 15**, **HP 45**, Speed 50 ft
- **Bite +5**, 1d8+3 piercing + 2d6 damage of a random type — roll d6 each attack: 1 fire, 2 cold, 3 lightning, 4 acid, 5 poison, 6 thunder
- **Chaos Breath (recharge 5-6):** 15-foot cone, DC 12 Dexterity save, 6d6 of the rolled type, half on a success
- **Pack Tactics**

Whimsy hunts whoever is currently transformed — it has been trained to. This creates a genuinely mean feedback loop and you should lean into it.

### Trial Gladiators (Thug, CR 1/2) ×3
Condemned, promised commutation, and told nothing about the Fonts.

- **AC 11**, **HP 32**, Speed 30 ft
- **Multiattack:** two mace attacks, +4 to hit, 1d6+2 bludgeoning each
- **Pack Tactics**

They open in the shimmer zones deliberately — they've been ordered to stand there. After the first pulse, roll each one's form: **two on the Beast table, one on the Monstrosity table** (this is the Magister cheating, and the announcer should say so).

### Enemy Tactics
- The gladiators screen the Magister and try to shove PCs toward Fonts (contested Athletics).
- Whimsy focuses transformed PCs, using Pack Tactics with whatever the gladiators have become.
- Ilvane will *counterspell* the party's cleric before he'll counterspell anything else — he considers divine magic "untidy."
- Nobody on the enemy side attacks a Font. They are worth 2,000 gold each and the Magister will say so, loudly, the first time a PC swings at one.

## Round-by-Round Escalation

**Round 1.** Gates open. Gladiators advance to the Font ring and hold there. Ilvane opens with *polymorph* on the party's heaviest hitter from 50+ feet. Whimsy circles wide.

**Round 2.** Gladiators engage whoever came closest. Ilvane uses *telekinesis* to haul a PC toward a shimmer zone. **Announcer calls the coming pulse at the end of this round** — the crowd starts a countdown.

**Round 3 (end).** **FIRST PULSE.** Everyone within 20 feet of a Font saves. Roll forms for the gladiators now.

**Rounds 4-5.** Peak chaos. Two or three creatures on the floor are beasts. Whimsy's breath recharges. Ilvane repositions to keep line of sight while staying clear of the rings.

**Round 6 (end).** **SECOND PULSE.** By now the party either understands the Fonts or is losing badly; see *Balancing on the Fly* below.

**Rounds 7-8.** Ilvane bloodied. *Blur*, then flight if pressed. If a PC is still in a weak form he re-targets them to keep them there.

**Round 9 (end).** **THIRD PULSE**, if the bout is somehow still going. Most tables finish in round 7 or 8.

### Victory Condition
Magister Ilvane is defeated, yields, or escapes, **and** the floor is cleared of hostile creatures. Fonts left standing at the end keep pulsing during the victory lap — a wonderful final image if someone gets careless.

## Objectives and Rewards

### Primary Objective: Win the Trial
Defeat or drive off the Magister and clear the floor.
**Reward:** 250 gp per surviving gladiator from the Cassine Academy's purse, plus standing.

### Secondary Objective: Break a Font
Destroy at least one Font (AC 15, 30 HP).
**Reward:** A fistful of shimmer-glass shards — 40 gp each, 2d4 shards per Font, and an alchemist in the lower market will pay double if asked nicely. The academies will be furious, which is its own reward.

### Tertiary Objective: Win It Wearing Fur
Land a killing blow while transformed.
**Reward:** +3 [[../../mechanics/crowds_favor|Favor]] and a nickname from the announcer that will follow the PC for the rest of the campaign. Decide the nickname at the table, immediately.

## Crowd Favor Opportunities

- Land a kill while transformed: **+2**
- Voluntarily fail a shimmer save and use the new form's signature ability that same round (toad swallow, snake constrict, elk ram): **+2**
- Destroy a Font specifically to free a transformed ally within 15 feet: **+2**
- Break the Magister's concentration and free an ally: **+2**
- Finish the bout having never been transformed: **+2**
- Kill Whimsy while it is mid-breath (readied action): **+2**
- Shove an enemy into a shimmer zone in the round before a pulse: **+1**
- Grapple something with tentacles: **+1**, and the announcer will not stop talking about it
- Anything a bear does with Panache: **+1**

## GM Tips

- **Print the two form tables and put them on the table, face up.** Players should be able to see what they might become before they choose to gamble. Half the fun is the anticipation.
- **Pre-roll the gladiators' forms before the session.** Write them on an index card. Do not roll these live; it costs ninety seconds each time.
- **Say the quiet part out loud the first time someone shifts:** they keep their own mind, they can still spend Favor, they can still roll with Panache. Otherwise a player will sit back thinking they've lost their turn.
- **Track four numbers only:** three Font HP totals and the Magister's HP. Everything else is a stat card.
- **Call the pulse one round early, every time**, through the announcer. This fight is only fun if the shimmer is predictable and the *positioning* is the gamble.
- **Torgana in particular** should get polymorphed early. A barbarian with no rage and no weapon, who is suddenly a sixty-hit-point constrictor, is the best thirty seconds of this session. Give it to them.
- **Divine magic has no answer to transmutation here** — Father Crow has no *dispel magic* and no *greater restoration* at this level. That's intentional: without a wizard on the sand, the answer is violence against the Magister's concentration, and the party should have to find that out. If [[../../players/silas_vale|Silas Vale]] is one of the four, he *can* counterspell or dispel — see the GM setup for how that reshapes the fight.
- **Don't let the beast forms slow you down.** If a player dithers, remind them the crowd is watching and count down from five.

## Aftermath Seeds

- **Patron interest.** A professor of the Cassine Academy — not Ilvane, a rival — approaches with a research stipend and an unsettling amount of interest in how a particular PC's body "took the shape."
- **Rumor.** One of the Trial Gladiators never reverted. He is still in the pens, still a grick, and still, by the pen-boss's reckoning, a person.
- **Downtime.** A black-market vendor offers a *Stabilized Shimmer Draught*: one use, choose your beast form voluntarily, lasts an hour.
- **Lingering effect.** A PC who spent most of the bout transformed keeps a trace of it — dreams of running on four legs, a taste for raw meat, an ear that twitches at sounds nobody else hears. Cosmetic. Play it up.
- **Political.** If a Font was destroyed, the academies bill the arena. The arena bills Snak. Snak bills the party. This is a real conversation to have next session.

## Scaling Options

### Too Easy?
- Ilvane stops fleeing and fights to the end, using his 5th-level slot on *telekinesis* against whoever is winning.
- Add a fourth Font at dead center, so the safe spot stops being safe in round 4.
- Whimsy's breath recharges on 4-6.

### Too Hard?
- Ilvane can only concentrate on one *polymorph* and will not re-cast it on a PC who has already been freed — he moves on to a new subject, "for the data."
- Reduce Font HP to 20 so a single focused round drops one.
- The crowd throws a Font-breaker: a smith's hammer lands near a PC, granting advantage on attacks against objects for the rest of the bout.

### Too Little Chaos?
- Pulse at the end of rounds 2, 4, 6 and 8 instead.
- Let a stray pulse catch Whimsy. A chaos hound that is suddenly a giant toad is a gift.

### Too Much Chaos?
- Only the nearest Font pulses each time, rather than all three.
- Announce which Font is about to pulse, not just that a pulse is coming.
